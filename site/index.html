What "proven" means

Every verdict says where it came from.

✓ proven

The SMT solver (Z3) showed the contract holds for every input.

~ fuzzed

The solver could not decide, so the fuzzer ran the function on random inputs against the contract. The verdict says which checks were left unproven.

✗ refuted

An input breaks the contract, found by the solver or the fuzzer, and printed with the verdict.

Termination is checked on its own: a loop or recursion with no bound shown is reported as may not terminate, never assumed to end. What is trusted: the solver, Strata's encoding of the program for it, the compiler and the C runtime. How verification works →
